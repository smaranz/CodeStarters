Our Mission

Teaching this generation AI & CS literacy — before the world leaves them behind.

Artificial intelligence is reshaping every industry. We ensure students in our community grow up not just as consumers of technology, but as builders of it.

What We Do

Two programs. One mission.

Everything we do is free for the communities we serve.

🧑‍💻

CS & AI Education

Hands-on workshops and after-school programs that teach K–12 students programming, machine learning, and AI concepts in an accessible, engaging way.

🌐

Free Websites for Local Businesses

Our student developers build and deploy professional websites for small Cupertino businesses at no cost — real-world experience for students, real value for the community.
